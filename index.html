The American health care system requires one visit with a “regular” doctor so you can get a referral to the “actual one” you need to meet. Of course the visit with a regular doctor didn't answer any questions. They gave me a referral and that was it.

Myvisit with Danielle Clair is on Dec 15, 2020 which is a week and a half after the referral. She points to muscle spasm as the cause for my problem and assumes that it will get better after a while.

Almost a month passed and my body still feels broken. So I was referred to aphysical therapist named Sarah Patton on Jan 07, 2021. But her institution provides PT that is not specific enough for my problem. I cancel the future appointments.

The doctor is struggling to identify what is wrong with me. Another office visit with Danielle Clair on Feb 10, 2021. The sad thing about doctors is that they are obligated to act like they know everything. They will never tell you "I don't know what's happening." Instead they will say "let's wait another month."

I am confused but never hopeless. The doctors know best. They always have, so far in my life.




Two months pass.

Apr 13, 2021 was my second to last visit with Danielle Clair because she finally accepts that she's not the person who can figure this out. She refers me to a specialist who treats the one condition she was suspecting. That appiontment comes 4 months after this visit.










Now I'm sitting in my first visit with Katrina Nicandri on Aug 19, 2021 . She finally gives me the diagnosis of my condition. Now the pain has a name, a plan, and a future. See? Doctors know best, they always do.
